Guesthouse at 2,400 m

A house above the salt flats

Four rooms facing west, a long table, and nothing between you and the evening but sixty kilometres of white ground.

Four rooms. One long window.

Built from the pale stone it stands on, Halde opens at five for the light and closes at ten for the stars. Meals come from the valley farms below, carried up twice a week.
