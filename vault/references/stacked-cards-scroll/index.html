Money stuff for people who share a fridge.

Kettle splits bills, rent and the weekly shop between housemates, and nobody has to be the one who asks.

    Bills split themselves

    Connect the energy, broadband and water accounts once. Kettle divides each bill the day it lands, by room size or evenly.
