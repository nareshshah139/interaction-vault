I. The slow block

We walked forty streets in a week and kept a notebook for each one. Most entries were short. A bakery that opened at five. A balcony with a single chair facing the wrong way. The notes were never about the buildings. They were about the pace a street asked of you, and whether you gave it.

Photographs withheld at the author's request.

Nothing on the map told us where to slow down. The pavement did.
